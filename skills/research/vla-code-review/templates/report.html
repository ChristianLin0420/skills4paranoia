FINDINGS — BY SEVERITY

BLOCKER4.4 Action-head gradients are not synchronised across ranks in multi-GPU training train.py:388
Symptom
No error. Multi-GPU and single-GPU loss curves look nearly identical, but multi-GPU underperforms and the gap widens with device count.
Cause
The action head is wrapped in DistributedDataParallel, but the code calls action_head.module.predict_action(...). Bypassing DDP.forward() leaves the reducer unregistered, so gradients never all-reduce on the backward pass. Each rank updates its own head from its local batch alone.
How to confirm
After a few steps, compare hashes of the action head's parameters across two ranks. With synchronisation working they must match.
Fix
Call action_head(...) rather than action_head.module(...); move the predict_action logic into forward().
Precedent: moojink/openvla-oft#160 — OpenVLA-OFT's finetune.py has exactly this problem.
BLOCKER10.5 A failed checkpoint load returns an untrained model instead of raising policy/loader.py:64
Symptom
The call looks successful, nothing raises, and a normal policy object comes back. The only clue is a warning scrolled far up the log.
Why it is fatal
Any validation comparing the loaded model against itself passes, because both sides are the same untrained model — the numbers agree perfectly and mean nothing. A whole batch of measurements can be worthless before you notice.
How to confirm
Compare a weight hash against the checkpoint file after loading, or assert that missing_keys and unexpected_keys are both empty.
Fix
Always raise on a failed load. To tolerate missing keys, list the permitted names explicitly rather than relying on a lenient default.
Precedent: huggingface/lerobot#4577 — PI0Policy.from_pretrained returns an untrained model even with strict=True.
HIGH2.1 Pure bf16 training with no fp32 master weights train.py:118
Symptom
Stops improving late in training while grad norm looks entirely normal. Easily misread as the method saturating.
Cause
bf16 has about 2^-8 relative precision. When |lr × grad| / |w| < 2^-8 the update leaves the weight unchanged and is silently discarded. Late in training weights grow and gradients shrink, so the condition is easy to satisfy.
How to confirm
Sample parameters and count the fraction unchanged across optimizer.step(). Anything above single-digit percent is a problem.
Fix
Switch to AMP, which keeps fp32 master weights by default, or add stochastic rounding or Kahan-compensated summation inside the optimizer.
Precedent: Physical-Intelligence/openpi#989 — same data and protocol: 82.6 with JAX mixed precision against 71.9 with pure-bf16 PyTorch.
HIGH6.8 One env is shared across the whole eval suite, so scene geometry drifts between episodes eval/run_eval.py:148
Symptom
The same checkpoint scores differently on two runs, and later episodes are less stable.
Cause
env.seed() is called once at construction, so the RNG stream advances across episodes. env.reset() samples fixture placement from that stream into sim.model.body_pos, while set_init_state() restores only qpos and qvel, not body_pos.
How to confirm
Diff sim.model.body_pos between consecutive episodes. It should be zero.
Fix
Call env.seed(cfg.seed) inside the episode loop, or rebuild the env per episode.
Precedent: openvla/openvla#342 — the stove moves 3.0mm between episodes during LIBERO evaluation.
HIGH9.1 No gradient clipping, and the pre-clip grad norm is not logged train.py:214
Symptom
A loss spike mid-training followed by collapse that never recovers. Output degenerates into a fixed action unrelated to the observation.
Why it matters
LoRA finetuning of VLAs is especially exposed: one spike is enough to write off the whole run, and you only find out at the end.
Fix
Add clip_grad_norm_, and log the norm both before and after clipping. Logging only the post-clip value pins it to the threshold and shows no warning at all.
Precedent: openvla/openvla#333 — LoRA finetuning: loss jumps from 2.2 to 5.2 at step 6000, success falls to zero and never recovers.
MEDIUM6.4 Action normalisation statistics are not bound to the checkpoint data/stats.py:23
Symptom
After a data version change the inference action magnitudes are wrong, while training metrics look entirely normal.
Fix
Store mean/std in the checkpoint and read them from it at inference. Assert at startup that the statistics hash matches training.
Precedent: moojink/openvla-oft#156, openpi#1025 (a community request for a normalisation compatibility report)。
WATCH6.7 The success criterion may be too loose — needs your judgement envs/tasks/place.py:88
What was seen
Success requires the object's centre inside the target box below a velocity threshold, but does not require the gripper to have released, and has no hold-duration requirement.
Why it is raised
Criteria of this shape have been shown to over-report on other benchmarks: success declared while the object is still in the gripper, or about to fall. I cannot judge whether that is acceptable for your task, so this is a watch rather than a finding.
Suggestion
Add a hold duration (say 10 consecutive frames) and a gripper-state condition, then re-run an old checkpoint and see how far the score moves. If it moves a lot, every past number needs reinterpreting.
Precedent: SimplerEnv#129 (WidowX tasks over-optimistic), LIBERO#149 (success before the object touches the table)。
