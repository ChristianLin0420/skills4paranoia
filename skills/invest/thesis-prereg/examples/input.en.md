# What you hand the skill

> **⚠ Mock.** Aldergrove Industrial does not exist. What is real is the shape of the
> request — including the closing "what do you think of this thesis", and the fact that
> the output **does not answer it**.

Real inputs are this untidy. The skill does not need you to have thought it through — its
job is turning "I think there is something here" into a document that can be shown wrong.

> I've been looking at an industrial distributor, Aldergrove.
>
> On the whole it's a declining distribution business — 7× earnings, 19% gross margin and
> falling. On a screen it looks like a melting ice cube.
>
> But going through their MD&A there's a service business inside it that's growing fast,
> and I'd guess 40%+ gross margin — except it's **never disclosed separately**, it's all
> inside "Industrial Solutions". So no screener or quant model can see it. Sell-side is one
> analyst at a regional broker.
>
> My thinking is: once the service piece gets big enough, they'll have to break it out, and
> that's when the market re-rates it. Buying now is buying something nobody can see yet.
> Classic good-business-hidden-inside-a-bad-one.
>
> Looking to hold about three years. **What do you think of the thesis?**
